Originally built for the "Anonymous" Discord community's own MP sessions, this mod keeps the core vanilla feel intact while fixing long-standing annoyances, adding quality-of-life tools, and expanding a handful of underdeveloped focus trees - all without turning the game into a total conversion.

What's in it

Soviet Union & Republics - expanded content
Soviet Republics are finally playable! With not big, but still a focus tree and with that Second Russian Civil War was reworked!

Rebalanced focus trees, worldwide
A large number of nations have had their focus trees reworked to make more countries genuinely fun and viable in multiplayer, not just the usual majors. Specific changes include:

Iraq, Iran, Afghanistan, Turkey, Philippines, Mexico, Belgium, Netherlands, Congo - rebalanced focuses
Iceland - removed a mutual-exclusivity restriction in the industry branch and doubled the buffs from its focuses
Spain - expanded focus tree from mod Spain: Extended
Poland - buffed Sanacija path
Greece - reworked right-wing branch
Denmark & Norway - buffed focuses
Yugoslavia - less punishing focuses (no more 1260 days Croatia opposition!)
Finland - slightly buffed industry
New Zealand - inherited Australia's older focus branch as an addition
Baltic states (Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia) - new shared Baltic Entente branch
Democratic countries of Europe - new shared European Union branch
Plus continued work on: USA, USSR, Germany, France, Italy, UK, Sweden, Romania

Research overhaul
Every country gets +2 extra research slots, and the tech tree itself has been tweaked so historically underused equipment - motorized artillery, armored cars - is actually fun to use now!

Smarter AI
AI behavior has been reworked to be more agreeable when players issue ultimatums, and to actually prioritize completing industry-focused national focuses, so multiplayer sessions don't grind to a halt waiting on a stubborn or unproductive AI neighbor.

Multiplayer quality-of-life:
No more puppets shoving their divisions onto you
Militia divisions available to every nation
Recall your volunteers whenever you want
Undeletable division templates (USSR, Italy, Switzerland, etc.) made fully editable
Faster minimum game speed, faster focus/research, more building slots
Added hotkey for naval invasion landings
Supply hubs decreased to 10,000 cost
Cleaner combat view, better map modes, minimalist battle plans, consistent modifier icons
Assorted interface fixes: no ads, no "Verify Game Files" tab

Cosmetic
New focus icons for Romania, Poland, and the UK; new ideology icons for Ukraine; a Portugal GFX pass; and added voice lines for dozens of countries that don't have any in vanilla.

Compatibility
Built and tested for our own MP ruleset - see the full list of integrated mods and credits below. Should be compatible with most other content mods; conflicts are most likely with other total-conversion overhauls.
